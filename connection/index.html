About the session

AI is no longer just a technology discussion. It's a business strategy discussion. Yet many organizations are finding that moving from AI pilots to meaningful business outcomes is more challenging than expected.

Join Jamal Khan, Chief Growth and Innovation Officer and Head of the CNXN Helix® Center for Applied AI and Robotics, and Dr. Gaurav Khanna, AI Leader, Cisco, for a practical conversation on what it really takes to build an AI-ready enterprise. Together, they'll share insights on aligning AI initiatives with business priorities, establishing the right foundation for success, and overcoming the challenges that often prevent organizations from scaling AI effectively.

Whether you're just beginning your AI journey or looking to expand existing initiatives, this session will provide practical guidance on building a strategy that delivers measurable business value.

Our approach · CNXN Helix®

A practical path from AI interest to AI outcomes

AI success does not happen all at once. The session follows the same structured process Connection uses to connect strategy to execution.

01

Discover

Goals, challenges, stakeholders, and current AI priorities.

02

Assess

Data, infrastructure, security, workflows, governance, readiness.

03

Design

Roadmap, architecture, partner approach, success measures.

04

Deploy

Pilots, workshops, platforms, automation, applied AI.

05

Scale

Early wins into repeatable, governed, enterprise-ready adoption.

Most organizations stall between 04 and 05. That's where this conversation spends its time.

What you'll walk away with

4 takeaways
01

Aligning AI with business priorities

How to choose initiatives that map to outcomes leadership already measures, instead of chasing the most exciting demo.

02

The right foundation for success

What data, infrastructure, security, and governance need to be in place before AI workloads can perform and scale.

03

Getting past the pilot stall

The challenges that most often prevent organizations from scaling AI effectively, and how teams are working through them.

04

A strategy that shows measurable value

Practical guidance whether you're just beginning your AI journey or expanding initiatives already in flight.
