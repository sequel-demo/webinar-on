Federation, governance, and change at scale.

That is who turns up to these sessions. The hard part was never getting engineers interested in Federation. It is keeping the conversation, and the recording, somewhere your team actually controls.

The session

The State of Federation 2026 survey captures how organizations adopt, operate, and scale Federated GraphQL architectures.

Your input shapes an unbiased, vendor-agnostic view of Federation in practice across organizations at every maturity stage. This session walks through what the survey is asking and why, then opens the floor to the engineers and platform teams living with those answers day to day.

Bring your own architecture. The most useful 45 minutes on this topic tends to be the one where somebody describes the thing that broke in production, and four other people recognise it immediately.

For every valid survey completed, WunderGraph donates $30 to UNICEF.

The mechanism

From scattered subgraphs to one governed supergraph

Schema changes get proposed, checked against composition, and promoted through the registry before anything reaches the router. Governance is a gate in the path, not a meeting after the fact.
