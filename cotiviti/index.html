But most plans aren’t losing ground on ambition — they’re losing it in the handoff.

Payment integrity and SIU teams routinely work the same providers, the same patterns, and the same dollars — from two different sides of the house. The gap between them is where waste and abuse survives.

About
this session

Prevention isn’t a tool. It’s a plan two teams agree on.

Effective waste and abuse prevention requires intentional planning, aligned goals, and a trusted partnership that connects payment integrity and SIU teams around a shared strategy.

Health plans need practical approaches for strengthening FWA prevention within a broader payment integrity strategy, including how payer teams can identify risk areas, apply structured governance, and use post-to-prepay feedback loops to support earlier interventions.

Join Cotiviti and health plan leaders as we explore how collaborative preparation and cross-functional coordination can help lay the groundwork for a more effective waste and abuse program.

The mechanism

From post-pay discovery to pre-pay prevention

The feedback loop is the whole argument. What SIU finds after the fact should be shaping what the plan stops before it pays — and that only happens when governance connects the two.

Step 01
Identify risk areas

Map where exposure actually sits across lines of business, providers, and claim types — before setting a single objective.

Step 02
Apply structured governance

Give the plan a cadence, an owner, and clear objectives, so prevention work survives contact with day-to-day operations.

Step 03
Align goals across teams

Payment integrity and SIU measured against one unified strategy instead of two scorecards that quietly compete.

Step 04
Close the post-to-prepay loop

Route confirmed post-pay findings back into pre-pay edits and reviews to support earlier, cheaper intervention.

The feedback loop
Claim submitted
Pre-pay edits & review
Post-pay / SIU investigation
Findings become new pre-pay rules

Each pass tightens the one before it. The earlier a pattern is caught, the less of it has to be chased down and recovered later.

What you’ll learn

Three things attendees will take back to their program

01
Build an intentional prevention plan

Identify risk areas, set clear objectives, and apply structured governance — so the plan is something the organization can actually run, not a slide.

02
Align goals across teams

Drive a unified payment integrity strategy where SIU and PI are pulling toward the same outcome instead of overlapping on the same providers.

03
Create and leverage trusted partnerships

Collaborative preparation, shared insights, and joint decision-making — internally between teams, and externally with the partners who see the claims.
