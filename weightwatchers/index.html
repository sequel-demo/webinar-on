The Session

A better GLP-1 journey starts with more than the prescription. Medication is one part of it. The rest is what a person does on a Tuesday afternoon: how they eat, how they move, who they lean on, and what they ask their clinician.

In this live session, the WeightWatchers team walks through how the GLP-1 Success program pairs behavioral support with medication, what expert support looks like in practice, and how members use community to stay on track.

Bring your questions. There's time at the end for the ones you didn't expect to ask.
