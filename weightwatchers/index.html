The Session

Weight health isn’t one-size-fits-all. The right partner makes all the difference for your organization, whether or not you cover GLP-1 medications.

In this live session for HR and benefits leaders, the Weight Watchers for Business team walks through how one platform flexes to fit: individualized pathways that bring clinical, behavioral and community care together at scale.

  • Manage costs
  • Increase engagement
  • Improve health outcomes

A consultative approach that fits your priorities

Four building blocks, defined around your biggest weight health challenges.

Behavioral Programs

Scalable, science-proven digital program to support lifestyle-based and chronic conditions like diabetes, hyperlipidemia, and hypertension.

Virtual Clinic + Medication Management

Comprehensive clinical weight management with obesity-trained clinicians to deliver quality care and provide access to GLP-1 medications.¹

Coaches and Community

24/7 coaching and a highly active digital community with 200+ affinity groups to help individuals stay engaged, connected, and accountable.

Integrated Population Insights

Comprehensive reporting and program analytics across your population, plus access to real-time data trend analysis.
