About the session

Every new AI agent you ship is something a customer team has to learn.

Talkdesk is moving customers from pilot to production on autonomous agents, Copilot and the CXA platform. Each release means new workflows for supervisors, new habits for agents and new questions for admins. Enablement is how all of that actually lands.

In this live Talkdesk Academy session, the Customer Enablement team walks through how they run training as a program: live sessions on talkdesk.com, an on-demand library that builds itself, and a clear view of which customer accounts are engaging and which ones need a CSM.

Bring your questions. The second half is open Q&A, and every question asked feeds the next session's agenda.

How it works on talkdesk.com

One session. Every customer signal, back in your hands.

01

Go live on talkdesk.com

Customers join inside the Talkdesk experience they already trust. No separate platform, no download.

02

See who showed up

Who watched, how far they got, which polls they answered and what they asked, by customer account.

03

Publish to the Academy

The recording becomes on-demand training, clips and a recap without anyone re-recording a thing.

04

Flag accounts for CSMs

Accounts that skipped or dropped off land with their CSM the same day, with the questions they asked.

What you'll walk away with

Built for customer enablement, CS and support leaders rolling out AI agents across their customer base.

01

Treat enablement as a program

Move from a calendar of one-off trainings to a series customers come back to, with a clear path from first login to full adoption.

02

Know which accounts are ready

Use attendance, watch time and questions as adoption signals, so you can see account readiness before renewal conversations start.

03

Scale the Academy without re-recording

Turn each live session into on-demand training and short clips your customers can find on talkdesk.com and in Talkdesk Academy.

04

Route the accounts that need help to the right owner the same day, with context on what they watched and asked.
