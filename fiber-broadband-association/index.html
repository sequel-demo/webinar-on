This Episode

Scaling Fiber in a Competitive Era

In this episode of Fiber for Breakfast, Gary Bolton, President & CEO of the Fiber Broadband Association, sits down with Gaurav Juneja, CEO of Fidium, to discuss the company’s next phase as a fiber‑first operator. With a network spanning more than 76,000 fiber route miles, Juneja shares how Fidium is accelerating expansion, enhancing customer experience, and leveraging new capital strategies to scale.

The conversation also explores Fidium’s push into wholesale and data center connectivity, its rural market advantage, and what these moves signal for the telecom landscape as competition intensifies and fiber investment surges.
