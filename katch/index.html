About the session

Health plans are under pressure to reduce medical costs without disrupting the member or provider experience.

But often, payment responsibility isn’t verified until after claims are paid, leaving money on the table.

This live session builds on Katch’s latest white paper on Proactive Injury Coverage Coordination (PICC): an earlier, more effective approach that helps plans identify the right payer before payment occurs. By aligning responsibility sooner, PICC helps prevent incorrect payments, reduce unnecessary denials, and unlock real, measurable value.

We’ll cover why PICC matters now, how it works alongside traditional subrogation, and what the results look like for plans that adopt it.

*Results are state and line of business dependent.

The shift left in payment coordination

Katch identifies, coordinates, and resolves payment responsibility, before and after claims are paid.

True end-to-end payment coordination, in four moves. The session walks through each one.

01 · Identify

Injury claim arrives

An accident-related claim enters the plan, and responsibility is still unverified.

02 · PICC

Right payer, first

Coverage is coordinated and the claim is redirected to the responsible payer before payment is made.

03 · Subrogation

Recover the rest

Subrogation experts and a nationwide legal network recover what was already paid.

04 · Resolve

Value returned

Money goes back where it belongs, with fewer member touchpoints along the way.
