About the session

Prevention requires intent in the moment.

Most endpoint tools were built to answer one question after the fact: what happened? That worked when the risky actor was malware. It breaks down when the actor is a trusted employee pasting data into an unapproved AI tool, an agent running a workflow nobody reviewed, or a user following a ClickFix prompt they were socially engineered into.

In this session, the Ent team walks through how an intent-aware endpoint reads user and agent activity in real time, tells normal work from risky actions, AI misuse, and mistakes, and steps in at the moment of risk, not days later. Expect live examples, the timeline as it builds, and the decisions a security team actually has to make.
