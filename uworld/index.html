The session

Everything you need to evaluate the 3-Day NCLEX Review — in 30 minutes.

A 30-minute session built to give nursing leaders everything they need to evaluate the 3-Day NCLEX Review with confidence: a walkthrough of the 3-day agenda, all three delivery formats, and real feedback from programs like yours, so you can make the right call for your December cohort before your fall planning window closes.

Deliver an engaging, interactive NCLEX final review without increasing faculty workload.

Twenty-four 45-minute modules delivered across three days — 18 total hours covering all four NCLEX Client Needs categories. Every module is built around the CJMM framework with NGN practice throughout, and includes a printed student booklet. Available as an NCLEX live review in person or online, or in an on-demand format.

Same content. Same instructors. Three ways to fit your program.

How the three days run

Student success. Faculty relief. Institutional strength.

Eight 45-minute modules a day, sequenced so students build clinical judgment before they're asked to apply it — with zero faculty prep required.

Day 01

Foundations & safe care

Eight modules establishing the CJMM frame and the Safe and Effective Care Environment category.

Day 02

Physiological integrity

The largest content block, worked through NGN-style items with live instructor reasoning.

Day 03

Integration & test day

Health promotion, psychosocial integrity, and a final push on applying judgment under exam conditions.

All four client needs categories Safe & Effective Care Environment Health Promotion & Maintenance Psychosocial Integrity Physiological Integrity
