About
the session

The modern attack surface spans platforms, people, and places you don't control.

When risk develops in places teams can't observe, it flanks the organization before defenses engage. Most security programs answer that with more collection, and end up with more noise sitting in more queues.

The HNTR Platform takes a different route. It brings intelligence, AI, and cybersecurity experts together in one continuous loop of discovery, validation, and disruption, so intelligence doesn't stop at insight. It drives action, helping teams stop threats early and prevent real-world harm before it escalates.

In 45 minutes we'll walk the loop end to end using real detections, show what validation actually removes from the queue, and take the last step most platforms leave to you: the takedown.

One platform. One cycle.

Discover. Validate. Disrupt.

The loop doesn't end at a report. Each stage feeds the next, and the output of disruption becomes the next cycle's collection.
