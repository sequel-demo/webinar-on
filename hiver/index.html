Why this
session

Hiver brings email, chat, Slack, voice, SMS and every channel into one workspace — then layers in AI Agents that resolve multi-step, cross-system requests end to end. A refund isn't one action, it's a chain: confirm the details, pull the invoice, check the policy, post the credit, close the loop.

In this session we'll show exactly what that chain looks like from the inside — the same view your team would get on a real ticket, not a slide about one. We'll also cover how AI Copilot drafts the reply while the agent is still investigating, and how resolved tickets turn themselves into help-center articles afterward.
