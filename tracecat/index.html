Scale your security workforce with agents you own.

Legacy SOAR promised automation and delivered a maintenance job. Every playbook was brittle, every integration was a project, and the backlog grew faster than the team could write code for it.

In this session we build the other way around. Start from a prompt, end with a deterministic workflow: an agent that triages the alert, a case that collects the evidence, and an approval gate that keeps a human on the trigger for anything that touches production.

Everything runs on open source you can self-host in your own VPC, on-prem, or on Tracecat cloud. Your prompts and detections stay yours, and every prompt, tool call, and decision lands in an audit log you can read.
