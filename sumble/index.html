About the session45 min · Live Q&A

Most tools give you fragments of the account. Here's what the full picture looks like in practice.

Sumble gives GTM teams a structured view of each target account: teams, reporting lines, technologies, hiring activity and buying signals. Plenty of teams have seen the demo. Fewer have seen how the people who built it actually run their own go-to-market on top of it.

In this session, Sumble's marketing team opens up their own workflow. How they decide which accounts deserve attention this week, how they find the team inside an account that actually owns the problem, and how they turn signals like a new job post or a shift in tech usage into campaigns and events that land with the right people.

Expect real screens, real accounts and an honest look at what changed once the guesswork came out of their own pipeline.
