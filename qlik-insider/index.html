About the session

Make your data work for AI with trust, context, and the freedom to adapt.

The way organizations build, govern, and act on data is being rewritten by agentic AI. Data engineering is moving from human hands to AI collaboration. Analytics is being rebuilt for AI systems that act directly on data. And as AI takes on more autonomy, control over where it runs and how it’s governed decides who you can trust.

In this Qlik Insider, Qlik’s product leaders walk through the 2026 roadmap for AI-ready data and analytics: what’s shipping, what’s next, and how One Qlik brings it together on an open architecture that gives you choice over lock-in.
