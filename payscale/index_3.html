About the session

60 minutes, live Q&A, on-demand replay for every registrant.

Find the gaps before they get harder and more expensive to address.

As organizations head into 2027 planning, compensation leaders are balancing competing priorities: staying competitive in the market, maintaining internal equity, managing pay transparency expectations, and making the right adjustments without stretching already-limited budgets. The challenge is knowing where your compensation strategy may be most vulnerable before those issues become harder and more expensive to address.

Join Ruth Thomas and Matthew Whitson of Gallagher for a practical look at the compensation decisions that can create risk heading into 2027. We’ll explore how market movement, salary structures, pay equity, transparency, and targeted pay adjustments can affect your compensation strategy, and what leaders can do now to identify gaps and make more informed decisions.
