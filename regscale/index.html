The Session

CR26 is final and FedRAMP 20x is live. Join our webinar to see what 20x looks like in practice, and how RegScale helps you scale your multi-framework programs beyond.

Every CSP now owes the same things regardless of path: machine-readable evidence, quarterly Ongoing Certification Reports, Vulnerability Detection and Response, and structured change notifications. Rev 5 holders have a transition to plan without disrupting an active authorization. New entrants have a class to choose before they enter, not after.

This session works the problem backward from January 1, 2027 — what has to be true, and by when.

The Control Lifecycle

A live, audit-ready package — produced, not assembled

Continuous controls monitoring means the artifact is a by-product of the running program. Every phase feeds the next, and the package is always current.
