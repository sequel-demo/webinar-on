How to read this

  1. Sorting is the dropdown above the table. Twelve orderings, both directions on every numeric column, plus "No sorting" to drop back to registry order. The column the sort applies to is highlighted in the header.
  2. Source. ClinicalTrials.gov: phase includes Phase 2, interventional, industry lead sponsor, at least one US site, status recruiting or not yet recruiting. Enrollment and site counts are the sponsor's registered figures. Sponsor name and the NCT button both open the study record.
  3. Per site is enrollment divided by US site count. 405 of 1,046 trials sit above 20 patients per site, roughly where timelines start slipping.
  4. Filters are OR inside a group and AND across groups; the counts beside each option update to reflect the other filters you have on.

Two honesty notes. The rare-disease flag is keyword matching on indication text, not a curated ontology. Company size is not a lookup: "larger company" is a hand-flagged list of 197 sponsors; the rest are assumed clinical-stage rather than verified.

Coverage: 1,046 of the 1,058 qualifying trials, so 98.9% rather than all of it.
