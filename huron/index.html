The session

As AI transformation takes a top priority in the C-suite agenda, many forward-thinking companies are deploying a handful of individual use cases that promise to unlock meaningful value. Yet many are starting to feel that a use-case-by-use-case approach may be insufficient.

AI will drive paradigm shifts that create, destroy, and become fundamental to companies' business models, leaving winners and losers in its wake. In this webcast, Innosight's Ned Calder and Freddy Solis — authors of "Leading into the Age of AI" — offer practical guidance on moving beyond individual use cases to build sustainable competitive advantage with AI.
