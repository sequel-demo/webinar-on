About this session45 min · Live Q&A

Use AI. Shape AI. Enable AI.

At ZEISS, we both use and shape artificial intelligence while also enabling its continuous advancement.

AI is no longer just a vision. It is an integral part of almost all industries and areas of daily life, and generative AI has once again increased the speed of technological innovation. In this live session, ZEISS experts walk through how that potential turns into real breakthroughs for visionaries in science, industry, medicine, and society.

We start where AI itself starts: the optics from ZEISS Semiconductor Manufacturing Technology that make microchips smaller, more powerful, and more energy efficient. From there we follow AI into the clinic, the factory floor, and the research lab, with live examples and time for your questions.

For us, AI is not an abstract technology. It is a tool that helps generate insights, optimize processes, and improve decisions, used safely, responsibly, and with real benefits for people.
