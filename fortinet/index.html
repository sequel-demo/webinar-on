Shadow AI is already in your environment

AI is rapidly changing the way employees work, collaborate, and access information. But as organizations embrace AI, security teams are facing new challenges — from shadow AI and sensitive data exposure to endpoint risk and operational complexity.

Join Fortinet experts to learn how organizations can securely enable AI adoption without sacrificing visibility, control, or productivity. You'll discover the latest innovations in FortiEndpoint, Fortinet's unified endpoint platform, and see how a single solution can help you govern AI usage, protect sensitive data, reduce endpoint risk, and simplify day-to-day security operations.
