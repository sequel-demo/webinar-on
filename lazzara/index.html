About the conference

Advanced personal injury education, built for chiropractors.

Now in its third year, the Chiropractic & Legal Injury Conference brings Arizona chiropractors together with Lazzara Law Firm for a full day of expert speakers, 12 free continuing education credits, advanced personal injury education, and valuable networking.

Chiropractors are often the first to see what an accident really did to a patient. The conference is designed to help providers elevate their knowledge, their practice, and their patient outcomes, from the first adjustment through the life of an injury claim.
