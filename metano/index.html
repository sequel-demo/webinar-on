AI agents are spreading across every surface.

Every organization is on a journey to become agentic, which means AI agents are multiplying faster than you can see. Claude and Cursor on the endpoint. Bedrock, Google Cloud, and Azure agents in the enterprise. Copilots inside GitLab, Glean, GitHub, and ServiceNow, acting inside tools you already approved.

In this session we start where the riskiest agent lives: the endpoint. Then we follow coverage out to homegrown, cloud, and SaaS agents, and show what each one is made of: the harness, the MCP servers, the skills and packages underneath, the identities it carries, and what it can reach.

Breadth raises questions. Depth answers them.
